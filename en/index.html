The name

Ownizo comes from owner.

The name comes from the English word owner — the person who owns something. From day one, the vision has been to serve those who have something to protect: the owner of a home, a business, wealth built over many years, or simply a trip planned months ahead.

Being an owner means being responsible for what you have achieved. Ownizo exists to make that responsibility lighter — and each brand in the group takes this idea into its own territory: the owner of a policy, the owner of a property, the owner of a trip.
